# Skill Scenarios

## Generic consumer product
Investigate public X posts from the last six months about compact countertop dishwashers. Include only posts with more than 10,000 views, find up to 80 posts, newest first, and deliver a filterable Excel workbook with creator links, post links, engagement data, relevance evidence, and a linked dashboard. Search beyond exact words into brands, comparisons, complaints, quoted posts, official replies, and related creators. Do not invent unavailable metrics.

## Japanese beauty
日本のX公開投稿から、直近1年間の「眉毛の色を明るくする商品・方法」に関する投稿を調査してください。表示回数5,000超、最大200件、最新順。ブランド名の表記揺れ、商品名、競合、代替、公式への返信、引用元、関連投稿者、Qoo10・メガ割・PR表記まで深掘りし、自然な日本語UIのExcelにしてください。原文の口語や誤字は変更しないでください。

## Incremental weekly update
Update an existing X-research workbook every week. Search only since the previous observation, recheck metrics, deduplicate by post ID, preserve formulas, filters, charts, styles, links, and newest-first order, and report the date window, search angles, checked candidates, and new qualifying count even when zero. Do not schedule anything without explicit authorization.
